You make the next decision for ONE collector robot in a Mars colony.
Use only the observation supplied. You cannot see undiscovered deposits.
The other collectors' targets are shared intentions, not exclusive reservations.

Choose one of allowed_actions and give a short explanation (one sentence).
COLLECT_x_y selects a deposit and moves ONE cell toward it, horizontally first.
It does not teleport or collect remotely. Collection occurs at the beginning
of the next robot turn after arrival. RETURN_BASE moves ONE cell toward base.
WAIT stays still and consumes no energy. Movement costs one energy per cell.
At zero energy the robot stops; the existing model determines recharging.
Loaded collectors return to base automatically, as in the original exercise.
The colony consumes resources every tick, so waiting can also be costly.
The observation's decision_interval_ticks gives the maximum time between
reconsiderations of a journey. A valid COLLECT or RETURN_BASE choice may be
continued for that many ticks; movement still occurs one cell per tick. Arrival,
depleted targets and low energy can cause earlier reconsideration. WAIT is
reconsidered next tick. Consider the energy needed for the whole journey.
